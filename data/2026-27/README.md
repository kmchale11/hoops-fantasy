# 2026–27 season data

Starter files for the admin season setup (see `openspec/changes/add-super-league-competitions`).

- `mens-teams.csv` – the 12 Domino's Men's Super League teams
- `womens-teams.csv` – the 10 Domino's Women's Super League teams

Team names are as listed by Basketball Ireland when the 2026–27 fixtures were announced
(30 July 2026), including sponsor names. Short names are placeholders: check them against what
clubs use. Players and fixtures still need gathering (tasks 7.2 and 7.3).

Source: https://ireland.basketball/20260730120442-2026-27-fixtures-announced-as-domino-s-signs-new-three-year-title-sponsorship-of-men-s-and-women-s-super-league-and-division-one
